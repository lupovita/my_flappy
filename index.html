02 / 04

A very small brain

Our network has two hidden layers with four neurons each. Small enough to draw, but enough to discover useful behavior.

5 signals→4 neurons→4 neurons→1 decision

03 / 04

Weights are tiny opinions

Each connection has a weight. A positive weight encourages a neuron; a negative one discourages it. Every neuron also has a bias.

signal = activation( inputs × weights + bias )
04 / 04

One answer: flap or wait

The last neuron produces a number from 0 to 1. Above 0.5 means FLAP; otherwise the bird waits.

0.73≥ 0.5FLAP!
01 / 04

Start with many random birds

Each bird gets a randomly filled network. Most are terrible at first—and that is exactly where learning begins.

🐤 🐤 🐤 🐤 🐤
02 / 04

Fitness is the score

Let every bird play. Its fitness is how far it gets (or how many pipes it passes). Higher is better.

🐤 2🐤 7🐤 12 ★
03 / 04

The best three survive

Keep the top three networks. They become the parents for the next generation instead of starting from scratch.

🏆 🏆 🏆 survive
04 / 04

Copy, mix, mutate, repeat

Children inherit parent weights, with a few small random changes. Over many generations, lucky improvements accumulate.

parents → offspring → play → select ↻
01 / 04

Keep the best three

After five races, rank every bird by total fitness. The top three networks survive unchanged, so strong ideas are never accidentally lost.

🥇 🥈 🥉 the parents
02 / 04

Same course, fair comparison

For the first five generations, every flock races the same five obstacle layouts. This removes luck: a higher score is much more likely to mean a better brain.

generations 1–5: fixed courses → fair fitness
03 / 04

Then raise the challenge

During every race, gaps shrink, pipes move faster, spacing tightens, and gravity grows stronger. Obstacle speed is also an input, so birds can adapt their timing.

easy → faster → tighter → harder
04 / 04

Mutation is a balancing act

Gentle mutation protects good behavior and improves steadily, but can get stuck. Wild mutation explores more possibilities, but may erase useful progress. Try both knobs in the Arena.

gentle = refine↔wild = explore
